SQL Business & Sales Data Analysis
============================================================================

Project Overview
------------------------------
This project analyzes sales transaction data using PostgreSQL to identify
product performance, category performance, monthly sales trends, city-level
sales, payment method performance, customer sales, and transaction status.

Project Objective
-----------------------------
The objective of this project is to use PostgreSQL and SQL to analyze
sales transaction data, identify important sales patterns, and generate
business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

Key Analysis Areas
--------------------------------
- Product sales performance
- Category performance
- Monthly sales trends
- City-level sales performance
- Payment method analysis
- Top customer analysis
- Transaction status analysis

Tools & Technologies
------------------------------------
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- pgAdmin 4

Dataset
--------------
The dataset contains 80 sales transactions with information about:
Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer
- City
- Product-
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Sales
- Payment Method
- Transaction Status


Business Questions
---------------------------------
The analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

1. What are the total sales generated by each product?
2. Which products have the highest number of transactions?
3. What is the average sales value per transaction for each product?
4. Which product category generates the highest completed sales?
5. How do completed sales vary across different months?
6. How do completed sales vary across product categories each month?
7. Which cities generate the highest completed sales?
8. Which payment methods generate the highest completed sales?
9. Which payment methods have the highest number of completed transactions?
10. Who are the top 5 customers based on completed sales?
11. How do transaction count and sales value vary across transaction statuses?

Key Business Insights
--------------------------------------
1. Laptops generated the highest total sales at ₹8.93 lakh, followed by
   smartphones at ₹4.95 lakh and tablets at ₹4.58 lakh.

2. Headphones recorded the highest number of transactions at 14, while
   laptops generated the highest total sales despite having only 10
   transactions.

3. Electronics generated ₹18.34 lakh in completed sales, substantially
   exceeding Accessories at ₹1.71 lakh.

4. Electronics generated higher completed sales than Accessories in every
   month from January to September. Electronics reached its highest monthly
   sales in March at ₹2.49 lakh.

5. Bengaluru recorded the highest completed sales at ₹4.34 lakh, while
   Jaipur recorded the lowest at ₹2.27 lakh among the cities analyzed.

6. Card generated the highest completed sales at ₹9.85 lakh, followed by
   UPI at ₹9.20 lakh, while Cash accounted for ₹1.00 lakh.

7. Devansh Rao and Yash Arora recorded the highest completed customer sales
   at ₹1.36 lakh each. The dataset contained 75 completed transactions,
   2 cancelled transactions, and 3 returned transactions.
